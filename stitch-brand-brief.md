## BASE BRAND PROMPT (paste this every time)

```
Brand: Activo Medical Marketing
Industry: Medical marketing agency serving doctors and clinics in Tijuana and Mexico
Language: Spanish (Mexico). Brand name stays in English.
Audience: Doctors, dentists, plastic surgeons, and medical specialists in Mexico

Visual style: Dark, cinematic, premium. Deep navy backgrounds with electric teal and violet accents. Modern and clinical without feeling cold. Confident, not flashy. The visual language says "expert agency", not "startup" or "generic healthcare".

Primary colors:
- Deep Navy (background): #051126
- Mid Navy (secondary surfaces): #0c1f3d
- Teal (primary brand / CTA): #007f97
- Electric Violet (accent / gradient endpoint): #5533ff
- Cyan (gradient endpoint for buttons): #00c4d4
- Indigo (depth accent): #4c3f77
- Mauve (warm depth accent): #7c587f
- Sage (cool neutral): #a4bcbc
- Green (success / metrics): #2bcb74
- Body text on light: #324a63

Signature gradients:
- Main CTA / hero accent: teal #007f97 → electric violet #5533ff (left to right or 135deg)
- Secondary button: teal #007f97 → cyan #00c4d4
- Kicker / label text: gradient #007f97 → #5533ff clipped to text
- Section CTA background: teal #007f97 → indigo #4c3f77

Typography:
- Primary font: Maven Pro (geometric sans-serif, bold weights for headlines)
- Display / impact font: FF Mark Pro Black (for maximum-weight hero text only)
- Headlines: very bold (800 weight), short and direct
- Body: medium weight (500), clean and readable

Shapes and surfaces:
- Pill-shaped buttons and badges (border-radius: 999px)
- Cards with 18px border radius and subtle shadow
- Glass/frosted overlays on dark hero surfaces (backdrop-filter blur, low-opacity white)
- Decorative radial glow orbs in teal and violet on dark sections
- No hard borders, no skeuomorphic elements

Logo: "ACTIVO" wordmark with a geometric arrow/triangle in the A, followed by "MEDICAL MARKETING" in a lighter weight below. White version on dark backgrounds.

Tone: Expert. Reassuring. Data-driven. Professional without being sterile. Like a high-end B2B tech brand applied to healthcare.
```

## BRAND DON'TS (tell Stitch what to avoid)

```
Do NOT use:
- Bright white backgrounds (off-white or navy only)
- Clipart or generic medical icons (stethoscopes, red crosses)
- Stock photo style imagery of random patients
- Sans-serif fonts that are not geometric (no Roboto, no Open Sans feel)
- Pastel or baby-blue healthcare color schemes
- Neon green or orange accents
- Rounded bubbly or playful typography
- Multiple competing accent colors in one design — maximum 2 per piece
```
